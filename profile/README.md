## webuildstuff.io

Independent software studio & project lab — we design, build, and operate small consumer web products, mostly free calculators backed by public data or published research.

🌐 [webuildstuff.io](https://webuildstuff.io) · 📬 support@webuildstuff.io · 📍 Toronto, Canada

### Live Products

- **[Drone Flight Calc](https://droneflightcalc.com)** — Plan drone flights with real-time environmental analysis
- **[upgrade.tools](https://upgrade.tools)** — Are your premium subscriptions actually saving you money?
- **[Toronto Crime Score](https://torontocrimescore.com)** — Toronto's safest and most dangerous neighbourhoods, ranked
- **[How Much Water Do I Need?](https://howmuchwaterdoineed.com)** — Daily water intake calculator built on 18 research-backed parameters
- **[PowerLoss.tech](https://powerloss.tech)** — See what charging inefficiencies cost you in bills, energy waste, and carbon
- **[goldoutput.com](https://goldoutput.com)** — Real-time gold output metrics for precious-metal investments
- **[EMF.exposed](https://emf.exposed)** — Estimate EMF exposure from phones, WiFi, and 5G with calculated safe distances

### Stack

Bun · TypeScript · Next.js & Vite · Cloudflare Workers · D1 · GitHub Actions on Ubicloud
